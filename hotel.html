About Rooftop Republic CTG

Rooftop Republic CTG is a boutique hotel perched above the walled city of Cartagena de Indias, Colombia. Four distinct stays — from the full-rooftop experience to intimate suites — all under one address.

Built for free spirits, night owls, and anyone who believes Cartagena deserves more than a chain hotel. Come as you are. Stay as long as you like.
