Our Story

About Rooftop Republic CTG

Rooftop Republic CTG is a boutique hotel in the heart of Cartagena de Indias, Colombia. We offer four distinct stays — from the full-mansion rooftop experience to intimate suites — all under one address.

Perched above the walled city with panoramic views of the Caribbean, every corner of Rooftop Republic CTG reflects what makes Cartagena unforgettable: warmth, color, and unfiltered energy.
