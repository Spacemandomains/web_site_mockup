Our Story

A Sanctuary Above
the Old City

Perched above Cartagena's legendary walled city, Rooftop Republic CTG is where Caribbean warmth meets boutique luxury. We offer an intimate escape designed for travelers who want to experience Colombia's most vibrant coastal city without sacrificing comfort or style.

From our signature rooftop terrace — complete with a pool and sweeping views of the old city's terracotta rooftops — to our thoughtfully appointed suites, every corner of Rooftop Republic CTG reflects the spirit of Cartagena: colorful, warm, and unforgettable.
